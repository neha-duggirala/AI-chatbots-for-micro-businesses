# ABHI MILK DAIRY — STANDARD POLICY DOCUMENT

---

### 1. What the Dairy Farm Provides

* **Pure & Unadulterated Milk:** 100% natural cow milk supplied directly as it comes from stress-free cows, completely free from antibiotics, synthetic growth hormones, preservatives, stabilizers, or added chemicals.
* **Farm-Level Chilling:** Milk is chilled immediately at the farm level using Bulk Milk Coolers to lock in natural nutrients and freshness.
* **Transparent Farming:** Humane, clean handling where cows have access to clean drinking water and healthy fodder.

### 2. Product Delivery Timeline & Reach

* **Transit Time:** It takes **24 to 36 hours** from the milking process at the farm to reach your doorstep, ensuring maximum freshness compared to conventional 72–96 hour supply chains.
* **Delivery Hours:** Daily morning doorstep delivery between **4:30 AM and 7:00 AM**.
* **Order Cut-Off:** Orders for next-day delivery must be placed before **9:00 PM**.
* **Service Locations:** Delivery is currently available across selected metropolitan service zones and nearby urban hubs. Shelf-stable items (such as Ghee) may be shipped nationwide.

### 3. Product Limitations & Handling Guidelines

* **No Artificial Preservatives:** Because the milk is non-homogenized, non-standardized, and contains zero chemical stabilizers, it may appear thin or natural in consistency.
* **Cold Chain Dependency:** The milk must be collected immediately upon delivery and refrigerated promptly. Leaving it un-refrigerated for extended periods may cause natural curdling due to the absence of artificial preservatives.
* **Shelf Life:** Best consumed fresh within **2 to 4 days** of delivery when stored under proper refrigeration (always check the "Use By" date printed on the package).

### 4. Returns & Quality Policy

* **Returns & Replacements:** Replacements or refunds are applicable only if the product is delivered damaged, unsealed, spoiled, expired, or incorrect.
* **Reporting:** Any delivery issues or defective items must be reported immediately to Customer Support along with the batch number and package details.
